How Electrostatic Charging Works

Conduction

Conduction occurs when charges physically transfer between objects that are in direct contact. When the charged rod touches the ball:

  • Electrons move from one object to the other
  • Both objects end up with the same type of charge
  • The original object loses some of its charge

Induction

Induction occurs without direct contact through charge redistribution:

  • A charged rod creates charge separation in a nearby object
  • If the object is grounded, electrons can flow in or out
  • When the ground is removed, the object retains an opposite charge
  • The original object maintains its full charge

Charge Distribution Visualization

In this simulation, you can see how charges distribute in the ball:

  • During induction, the ball shows charge separation (different colors on each side)
  • In conduction, the charge distributes uniformly (same color throughout)
  • The + and - symbols represent positive and negative charges
